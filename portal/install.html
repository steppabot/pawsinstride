Add Paws in Stride to your home screen

Open your portal in one tap, just like an app. It takes about 30 seconds.

You're all set

Paws in Stride is installed on this device. You can open it any time from your home screen.

Open this page in your browser first

This page is open inside another app, and those can't install anything. Tap the ••• or Share button and choose Open in Safari or Open in Chrome, then come back to these steps.

Your browser can install it for you. Tap the button and confirm.

  1. 1

    Tap the ••• button

    It sits in Safari's bottom bar, usually at the bottom right. On older iPhones there's a Share icon in the bottom bar instead. If you see that, tap it and skip to step 3.

    Newer iPhones

    Older iPhones

  2. 2

    Tap Share

    You'll find it in the menu that opens.

  3. 3

    Tap Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the list if you don't see it right away. You may need to tap View More.

  4. 4

    Keep “Open as Web App” on, then tap Add

    Leave the switch on so it opens like a real app.

  5. 5

    Open Paws in Stride from your home screen

    Look for the Paws in Stride icon. You may need to log in once the first time you open it.

Chrome, Edge and Firefox on iPhone can add it too. If you can't find these options, open this page in Safari and use the Safari steps.

  1. 1

    Tap the Share icon

    Look for the square with an arrow pointing up, next to the address bar. If you don't see it, tap the ••• menu and choose Share.

  2. 2

    Tap Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the list if you need to. You may need to tap View More.

  3. 3

    Keep “Open as Web App” on, then tap Add

    Leave the switch on so it opens like a real app.

  4. 4

    Open Paws in Stride from your home screen

    You may need to log in once the first time you open it.

  1. 1

  2. 2

    Tap Install app

    Some phones call it Add to Home screen. Both do the same thing.

  3. 3

    Tap Install

    Confirm in the pop-up.

  4. 4
