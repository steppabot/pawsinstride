Dog Walking Services

Our dog walking service is designed to help your dog stay active, burn energy, and enjoy a healthier daily routine while you’re away. Each walk is tailored to your dog’s pace, personality, and comfort level.

Every walk includes one-on-one attention, GPS-tracked updates, and photo/video check-ins so you always know how your dog is doing. Whether your dog needs a quick potty break or a longer walk, we focus on safe, dependable, personalized care.

Ideal for busy pet owners who want consistent exercise, structure, and midday support.

Recurring service: Dog walking services require a minimum recurring schedule of 3 days per week.

Drop-In Visits / Potty Breaks

Our drop-in visits are perfect for pets who need a potty break, feeding, fresh water, and a little extra attention while you’re away. Visits are available in 15, 30, or 60-minute options depending on your pet’s routine and needs.

During each visit, we can provide a potty break, companionship, playtime, feeding, fresh water, and a quick reset before you get home. You’ll also receive updates with photos and videos so you can feel confident your pet is being well cared for.

Great for workdays, busy schedules, and pets who need consistent midday attention.

Recurring service: Drop-in visits require a minimum recurring schedule of 3 days per week.

Pet Sitting

VIP Dog Boarding

Our VIP dog boarding service gives your dog a safe, comfortable home-style stay with personalized care and attention. Unlike large traditional boarding facilities, your dog receives more one-on-one attention, more comfort, and a less stressful stay.

Perfect for dogs who do better in a home environment rather than a crowded kennel setting.

Boarding includes potty breaks, feeding, daily walks, playtime, and regular updates so you can travel with peace of mind. We focus on making your dog feel secure, cared for, and as comfortable as possible while you're away.

Boarding hours: Drop-off is available beginning at 8:00 AM. Pick-up is required by 12:00 PM on the scheduled departure day.
