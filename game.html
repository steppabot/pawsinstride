A Paws In Stride game

Your 3-minute shift starts at Paws In Stride in downtown Frisco. You get paid for every dog you bring home happy.

  1. 1Follow the arrow to the client's door and pick up their dog.
  2. 2Walk the dog to the Bark Park at Simpson Plaza.
  3. 3Bring the dog home. You earn a walk fee, plus an on-time bonus for quick walks.
  4. $Earn $150 in one shift for real Paws In Stride account credit: $20 for new clients, $5 a month for current clients.
  5. +Swing by the Water Tower, City Hall, and the Public Library for a $5 landmark bonus each shift.

Drag anywhere to walk, or use the arrow keys or WASD. Tap Heel (or press Space) when your dog spots a squirrel. Scoop poop for a speed boost, because clients notice what you leave behind. Watch for cars.
