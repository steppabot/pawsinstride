About Paws in Stride

Hi, I’m Greg — the owner of Paws in Stride. I’ve been a dog owner for over 15 years and know how important it is to have someone you can trust caring for your dog. My own dog, Chi, is a big part of why I started this business.

After living in Frisco for over a decade, I saw how many busy dog owners needed reliable, consistent help during the day. Paws in Stride was built to provide dependable, personalized care — not just quick walks, but real attention and structure for your dog.

Every service is designed to give you peace of mind with real-time updates, photos, and a level of care that feels like family. My goal is simple — keep your dog happy, active, and well cared for while you’re away.

Our Commitment

Safety First: Your dog’s safety always comes first. Every walk is handled with care, awareness, and the right equipment so your pet stays secure and comfortable from start to finish.

Personalized Care: No two dogs are the same. We tailor each walk or visit to your dog’s energy level, routine, and personality so they get the kind of care that fits them best.

Reliable Communication: We keep you in the loop with photo and video updates so you know how your dog is doing while you’re away. You should never have to wonder how things went.

Dependable Service: When you book with Paws in Stride, you can count on consistency, professionalism, and genuine care. We show up on time, follow through, and treat your dog like family.
