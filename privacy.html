Paws in Stride

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Paws in Stride ("we", "our" or "us") provides dog walking, drop-in visits, pet sitting and boarding in Frisco, Texas and nearby cities. This policy explains what information we collect through our website (pawsinstride.com) and the Paws in Stride app for iPhone and Android, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

The short version: we only collect what we need to care for your pets and run your bookings. We never sell your information, we don't show ads, and the app doesn't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account yourself at any time in the app.

Information we collect

Your account and contact details

  • Your name, email address, phone number and password. Your password is stored by our sign-in provider in a protected (hashed) form; we can't see it.
  • An emergency contact, if you add one.
  • A profile photo, if you add one.

Your home

  • Your service address, and home notes and property access details you choose to give us, such as gate codes, lockbox or key instructions and alarm instructions. Only Paws in Stride and our team members who care for your pets can see these.

Your pets

  • Pet names, breeds, birthdays, genders and photos, and the feeding, medical and care notes you add.

Bookings, visits and messages

  • The services you book, dates, times, prices and visit status.
  • Visit reports, including notes and photos our team takes of your pets during a visit or stay.
  • Messages you send us and we send you in the app.

Walk routes and location

  • During a walk, the Paws in Stride team member's phone records the walk's GPS route. The route, distance and time are saved with the visit so you can see where your dog walked in the visit report. This uses our team member's location, not yours, and only while a walk is active. The app keeps recording an active walk when the phone is locked.
  • Team members' locations may also be used to plan the drive between scheduled visits.
  • The app does not collect your location as a client.

Payments and credit

  • Payments are handled by PayPal (PayPal, Venmo, card and Apple Pay payments). Your payment details go directly to PayPal; we never receive or store your full card details.
  • We keep records of what you paid, when, refunds, and any account credit.

Notifications

  • If you allow notifications, we store a notification token for your device so we can send you updates such as visit starts, finished visit reports, messages and booking changes. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Face ID sign-in (iPhone)

  • If you turn on Face ID sign-in, your sign-in details are saved only on your iPhone, in the iPhone's secure Keychain, and are unlocked with Face ID. Your Face ID data never leaves your iPhone and is never sent to us. You can turn it off at any time in Profile.

Our website

  • Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they view. Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you visit, your browser and device type, and your approximate location, using cookies. We don't use analytics or advertising tools in the Paws in Stride app.
  • If you contact us through our website, ads or social media, we collect what you send us, such as your name, contact details and information about your pets and service needs.
  • If you play the Leash Run game while signed in, we save your scores and any rewards you earn.

How we use your information

  • To schedule and provide the services you book, and to get into your home and care for your pets as you've instructed.
  • To send you visit updates, visit reports, photos and walk routes.
  • To process payments, refunds and account credit.
  • To answer your messages and provide customer support.
  • To keep our services safe and improve them.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the people and companies we need to run our services:

  • Our team. Team members who care for your pets see the details needed to do that, such as your address, access instructions, pet care notes and contact details.
  • Supabase hosts our secure database, sign-in system and photo storage.
  • PayPal processes PayPal, Venmo, card and Apple Pay payments, and may collect information about your device and how you use its payment forms to help prevent fraud.
  • Apple and Google deliver push notifications to your device (Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging), and web notifications go through your browser's notification service.
  • Google Maps shows walk routes on a map and gives our team directions to your home.
  • Google Analytics measures visits to our website, as described above.

Each of these companies only receives the information it needs to provide its service to us, and is required to protect it with the same or equal protection as described in this policy. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or pets.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account information, pets, photos, visit reports, walk routes and messages while your account is open, so you can look back at your pets' visits.
  • Records of past visits, payments and account credit are kept for our business, tax and accounting records. If you delete your account, we keep these records with your personal information removed.
  • Information you send us through our website or social media without creating an account is kept only as long as we need it to respond to you and provide any services you book.

Deleting your account and your information

You can delete your account yourself at any time: in the app or at pawsinstride.com/portal, go to Profile and tap Delete Account. Upcoming visits need to be cancelled first. Deleting your account permanently erases your name, contact details, address, emergency contact, home notes and access codes, your profile photo, pet photos and pet care details, your messages and notifications, and your login. Any remaining account credit is forfeited.

See Delete your account for the full details. If you can't sign in, or you'd like us to delete or correct any other information, email contact@pawsinstride.com from the email address on your account.

Your choices

  • You can view and update your contact details, home details and pets in the app at any time.
  • You can turn notifications and Face ID sign-in on or off.
  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser, or opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser add-on.

Security

Your information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls so that each client can only see their own account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will let you know if a problem affects you.

Children

Our services are for adults booking care for their pets. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
