Delete your account

You can delete your Paws in Stride account yourself at any time, from the app or the website.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Paws in Stride app, or sign in at pawsinstride.com/portal.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Under Account Security, tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Permanently Delete Account.

If you have upcoming visits, please cancel them first. Your account is deleted immediately and you'll be signed out.

Sign in to delete your account

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your home address, emergency contact, home notes and property access codes
  • Your profile photo, pet photos and pet care details
  • Your messages, notifications and notification settings
  • Your login. You won't be able to sign in with this account again.

What we keep

  • Records of past visits, payments and account credit, with your personal information removed. We keep these for our business, tax and accounting records.
  • Any remaining account credit is forfeited when the account is deleted.

Can't sign in?

Email contact@pawsinstride.com from the email address on your account and we'll delete it for you.
