8 Best Dog-Friendly Restaurants & Patios in Frisco, TX

By Paws in Stride · Updated September 2026

Frisco has a growing list of restaurants and cafes with patios where your dog is welcome to join you. Whether you're after a Saturday brunch, a casual taco run, or a beer on a shaded patio, there's a spot for it.

As a local dog walking company serving Frisco, we're often asked where clients can actually bring their dog along. Below are 8 Frisco restaurants with dog-friendly patios worth trying.

The short answer

For the easiest, most no-fuss option, start with Frisco Rail Yard — it's an entirely outdoor food truck park, so there's zero ambiguity about whether your dog can be there. For a laid-back brunch, try 5th Street Patio Cafe or The Common Table in the Star District. All of these restaurants only allow dogs on their outdoor patios, never inside.

Before you go: Texas law allows restaurants to permit leashed dogs in outdoor dining areas only if customers and dogs enter directly from outside (never by walking through the restaurant), the area is posted with a sign, and dogs stay off seats, tables, and countertops. Restaurants can set stricter rules than this or choose not to allow dogs at all, and policies can change, so it's worth calling ahead or checking a restaurant's social media before you go, especially on a busy weekend.

    5th Street Patio Cafe

    8621 5th Street, Frisco, TX 75034Directions

    Brunch & breakfast

    5th Street Patio Cafe is one of the older brunch spots in downtown Frisco, known for homemade breakfast plates, scrambles, and sandwiches. True to its name, it's built around a patio, which is dog-friendly.

    It's open for breakfast and lunch only (no dinner), so it's better suited to a morning walk with your dog than an evening outing.

    Why dog owners may like it

    A patio that's genuinely part of the restaurant's identity, in a walkable downtown area.

    Good for

    Weekend brunch and morning walks through downtown Frisco.

    Local tip: Hours are 7 a.m.–3:30 p.m. on weekdays and 8 a.m.–2:30 p.m. on weekends, so it's a breakfast-and-lunch stop, not a dinner one. Weekend mornings get busy, so arrive early for patio seating.

    Its outdoor patio is dog-friendly, and the Star District setting means there's usually plenty of foot traffic to make for good people- (and dog-) watching.

    Why dog owners may like it

    Good for

    Weekend brunch outings and dogs that don't mind some noise and activity nearby.

    Frisco Rail Yard

    9040 First Street, Frisco, TX 75034Directions

    Food truck park

    It's a good pick if you want more of a hangout than a sit-down meal, especially with live music some evenings.

    Why dog owners may like it

    Entirely outdoor seating, food truck variety, and live music or games to make an evening of it.

    Good for

    A relaxed hangout rather than a sit-down meal, and dogs that don't mind some noise and crowds.

Dog-friendly dining questions

Can dogs go inside restaurants in Frisco?

No. Texas law only allows dogs in outdoor dining areas that customers and dogs can reach directly from outside, never by walking through the restaurant's interior. Dogs (other than trained service animals) aren't allowed inside.

Do I need to call ahead before bringing my dog?

It's a good habit. Restaurants can choose not to allow dogs at all, or restrict it to certain hours or sections, and policies can change. A quick call or a look at the restaurant's social media before you go can save you a wasted trip.

Does my dog need to be on a leash?

Yes. Texas law requires the dog to be leashed and under the owner's control at all times in an outdoor dining area, and dogs aren't allowed on chairs, tables, or countertops.

What if my dog isn't used to restaurant patios?

Start with a quieter time of day and a shorter visit. Bring water, watch how your dog handles the noise and new smells, and be ready to leave early if they seem overwhelmed. A tired dog after a walk usually settles in more easily than one who's full of energy.
